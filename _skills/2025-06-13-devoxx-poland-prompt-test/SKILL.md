STRUCTURE. Problem-diagnosis-solution in four acts. Act 1 (1-8) asserts the thesis before proving it -- slide 2 carries "WE HAVE A TRUST PROBLEM" with no support, and the survey and three arXiv papers arrive afterwards as corroboration. Act 2 (9-29) widens the problem past AI: design documents are "Write-once, Read-maybe-once", curse of knowledge and vague responsibility boundaries already break intent transfer between humans, and slide 19 replays slide 2's exact image with the thesis extended -- "(NOT ONLY WITH AI)". VERIFIABLE INTENT"), the methodology named in the deck's only starburst, then a disciplined propose-and-reject rhythm (TDD, BDD, all-LLM pipeline) before the trust table walks each artifact from red X to green check. Act 4 (63-67) evaluates honestly, shows a product, quotes a tweet, summarizes.

The trust table is the engine and the thing worth stealing: ARTIFACT / CREATED / CAN WE TRUST IT, four rows, six slides (47, 50, 53, 55, 57, 59), one cell changing per iteration, a human emoji for what a person made and a monkey emoji for what an LLM made. By 59 the third column reads VERIFIED and every row is clean. One visual holding an entire argument.

SLIDE-TO-SPEECH. Two-layer construction with a real destination. Room layer: a Bangers headline in a tilted white card carrying the claim unaided. Reward layer: the actual arXiv papers at sub-room-legible size with red-boxed findings, the SDD template, the Cucumber Venn's labels, the Karpathy tweet's engagement counts. The destination is built in, not bolted on -- a shownotes slide with QR at position 8 and SPEAKING.JBARU.CH in the footer of 59 consecutive slides. That is second-look, scored moderate only because without a recording the pattern's own test (does the speaker narrate the fine detail?) is unanswerable, and the red boxes suggest he does.

## Areas for Improvement (Dimension 14)

The close under-delivers relative to the build. Act 3 spends thirty-three slides earning a verified trust chain; slide 66 gives it away as a four-bullet summary ending on "But there is still work to be done", and 67 is thanks plus handles. The deck executes the persuasive opening turning point beautifully (slide 30) and declines the closing one -- there is no executable ask anywhere, and nobody is told what to do on Monday. The speaker's own default is a three-part close (summary / CTA / thanks); here it collapses to two with the CTA reduced to a hashtag. Severity: warning.

bullet-riddled-corpse [CONTEXTUAL], warning. The default argumentative unit is a bulleted white card and the closing run is three back to back (62, 63, 66). Slides 21/22 and 61 stack five parallel bullets. Slide 61 shipped a garbled line -- "SDDs are Specs are living docs" -- which is what an unproofread speaker-note looks like on a projector.

injured-outlines [CONTEXTUAL], info. Slides 41, 49 and 54 each put a heading over a single-item list. Each is one sentence wearing two type sizes.

nodding-room [CONTEXTUAL], warning. The significant one, because the talk is otherwise good. It is teaching-shaped -- a methodology meant to be adopted -- and its only audience-production moments are the "TDD?" / "BDD?" thought bubbles. Every other question is answered by the deck within a slide or two, every callback is a restatement, and the close reads its own summary aloud. The trust table is a gift here and is not used as one: asking the room which cell turns green next, before turning it, costs nothing and converts six passive reveals into six commitments.

alienating-artifact [CONTEXTUAL], info. Slide 28 puts a Terminator labelled "AI CODING AGENTS" over a distressed anime girl labelled "SOFTWARE QUALITY". The threat metaphor is obviously the intent, but the composition reads before the metaphor does in a mixed room. Cheap to swap.

Two smaller items. The Michael Scott image is reused verbatim on 35 and 39 with its caption clipped off the bottom slide edge both times -- the punchline is physically cut in half, the only broken layout in the deck. And the arXiv evidence is spent rather than invested: three papers arrive on 4-6 and never return, so the strongest support does no work after minute three. Bringing one back at the trust table would cost one slide and convert the apparatus from costume to structure.

Underuse, not antipattern: no retrieval-beat, no new-bliss, no call-to-action -- three patterns that fit a methodology proposal precisely, all absent. Breadth is otherwise wide at 26 distinct patterns.
